# Project license options

No project license has been selected. Until the owner chooses and adds `LICENSE`, normal copyright restrictions apply.

| Option | Source distribution / modifications | Must publish changes | Patent terms | Commercial use | Owner action |
|---|---|---|---|---|---|
| MIT | Permitted | No | No explicit patent grant | Yes | Add copyright holder/year and MIT text |
| Apache-2.0 | Permitted | No | Explicit patent grant and termination terms | Yes | Add Apache text and any required NOTICE |
| GNU GPLv3 | Permitted under GPL | Yes, for distributed derivatives | Express patent provisions | Yes, with copyleft obligations | Add GPLv3 text and provide corresponding source |
| Proprietary / All Rights Reserved | Only as separately authorized | Not applicable | Defined by owner agreement | Yes, controlled by owner | Publish terms/EULA and contributor ownership policy |

The owner should confirm contributor rights, distribution goals and desired patent/copyright policy before selecting an option.
